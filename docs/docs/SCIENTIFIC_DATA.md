ARES-X – Scientific Data

Primary Data Sources

1. MOLA

- Full name: Mars Orbiter Laser Altimeter
- Purpose: Martian elevation and terrain analysis.
- Use: Elevation and topographic visualisation.

2. Viking MDIM21

- Purpose: Global Mars image mosaic.
- Use: Surface mapping and visual exploration.

3. NASA Mars Imagery

- Purpose: Real images and visual references of Mars.
- Use: Regional research and project demonstrations.

Additional Research Sources

- HiRISE – High-resolution orbital imagery.
- CTX – Context Camera imagery.
- THEMIS – Thermal observations.
- CRISM – Mineralogical and spectral observations.
- USGS – Planetary geological maps.

Primary Study Region

Jezero Crater, Mars.

Data Integrity

ARES-X distinguishes between integrated datasets, referenced research sources, derived calculations and simulations. A source is not considered integrated unless its data has actually been processed or displayed in the platform.
